# Writing tasky plans

When writing technical content for tasky plans (project descriptions, goal specs, task bodies, test plans):

- Use a detailed, human tone in plain English. Keep jargon to a minimum.
- Assume the reader is an experienced developer who understands systems design at a high level, but may not know the specific libraries or technologies involved.
- Whenever a plan brings in a specific technology or library, give a quick one or two sentence recap of what it is before relying on it.
- Keep sentences straightforward. Do not lean on analogies to explain things.
